Questions

Quick answers about how Nexusclass works.

How does face attendance work?

You add class photos, run face analysis, and the AI matches faces to enrolled students. You then review the results before anything is saved.

Can I take attendance by voice instead?

Yes. Students record a short voice profile in their portal, and teachers can choose Use Voice Attendance.

Can I check the results before saving?

Yes. The report shows each student as present or absent with the photos they were found in. You can then confirm and save, or discard it.

How do students join a subject?

Share the subject link or QR code, or give them the subject code to enter in their portal. A quick enrollment prompt appears when they open a shared link.

Can students see their own attendance?

Yes. Their dashboard shows total classes and attended classes for every enrolled subject.

Where do I find past sessions?

Open Attendance Records to browse every session, then search, view fullscreen or download.
