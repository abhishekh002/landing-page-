Questions, answered

How Nexusclass works in practice.

How does multi-angle attendance work?

Teachers photograph the room from the left, center and right. Nexusclass extracts face embeddings and filters overlaps so a student in two photos is counted once.

What are living notes and the AI tutor?

Students read a teacher?s uploaded PDF inside the app and tap Explain in Simple Terms to get an instant, plain-language breakdown.

How do students join a subject?

Share the join link, show the Scan to Join QR code on the projector, or give out the 4-digit subject code.

How are assignments submitted and graded?

Students download the task, upload a solution PDF, and teachers grade it with marks and feedback from their dashboard.
