Questions & Answers

Quick answers about how Nexusclass works in practice.

Where can I try the live working application?

You can access the deployed working application at nexusattendance.streamlit.app. Both the Teacher Dashboard and Student Learning Portal are live and functional.

How does multi-angle face attendance work?

Teachers take panoramic photos of the classroom (Left, Center, Right). Nexusclass extracts facial embeddings and runs a spatial deduplication filter to ensure students appearing in overlapping photos are counted only once.

Where can I watch the video walkthroughs?

You can watch the 3-minute Teacher Guide at youtu.be/kGXJbQGDl8Y and the 2-minute Student Guide at youtu.be/wMs9LyDJGs8.

What is the Living Note Reader and AI Tutor (ELI5)?

When a teacher uploads a course PDF, students can read it inside the app and click 'Explain in Simple Terms (ELI5)' to get an instant AI-powered conceptual breakdown of difficult topics.

How do students submit assignments and receive grades?

Students download assessment papers directly from their portal, upload their completed solution PDF, and teachers grade them with marks and feedback in their dashboard.

How do students join a subject?

Teachers can share a direct join link, display a "Scan to Join" QR code on the classroom projector, or provide a 4-digit subject code for students to enter in their portal.
